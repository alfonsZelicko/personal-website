## Education & Early Roots

**FIT VUT (Brno University of Technology)** | _Computer Science & Mathematics_

- **Core Computer Science:** Deep dive into algorithms and system-level thinking behind modern
  computing.
  > I was offered remote work alongside studies, but real-world engineering quickly took priority
  > over academia.

**College of Polytechnics Jihlava** | _Management and IT Studies_

- **Integrity First:** Studies ended after a conflict over system security and ethics, reinforcing
  my preference for competence over formal titles.
  > The choice was simple: compromise integrity or leave. I left.

**Secondary School of Graphic Arts (Jihlava)** | _Graphic Design_

- **Visual Perspective:** Built strong foundations in UI/UX, typography, and visual detail,
  complementing my technical background.
  > Realistic painting never worked for me—everything turns into a caricature.

**Secondary School of Industry (SPŠT Třebíč)** | _Electronic Computer Systems_

- **The Beginning:** Built my first chess engine and discovered a passion for logic and digital
  systems.
- **Achievement:** Winner of a national programming competition.
  > The strongest competitor was sick, but results stand.

### Honorable Mention: Primary School

**Primary School (ZŠ Valeč)** | _Critical Thinking Foundations_

- **Key Influence:** I had the privilege of being in the class of
  [Miloš Šlapal](https://www.kellnerfoundation.cz/pomahame-skolam-k-uspechu/lide/mgr-milos-slapal-clen-odborneho-tymu),
  now a renowned Czech expert on reading literacy and critical thinking.

  > It was his first school, and we were his “guinea pigs” for methods he later refined throughout
  > his career.

  > His unconventional, interdisciplinary teaching shaped how I question, analyze, and seek the
  > “why” behind facts... and my passion for books.
