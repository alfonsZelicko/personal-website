## Development Environment & Stack

My development philosophy centers on efficiency and deep integration. I prefer tools that work out of the box and allow me to focus on writing code rather than configuring the editor for hours.

### IDEs & Ecosystem

I am a dedicated fan of the JetBrains ecosystem. My primary tools include:

- WebStorm / IntelliJ IDEA / PyCharm – Depending on the project, these are my daily drivers.
- YouTrack & TeamCity – I value how beautifully these tools are interconnected, creating a seamless workflow from task tracking to CI/CD.

While I respect Visual Studio Code for its accessibility and browser-based capabilities, it remains my "backup" solution. I find it somewhat overrated for heavy development; where WebStorm is ready immediately after installation, VS Code often requires hours of plugin hunting to match the same level of productivity. (And yes, the lack of native middle-click support is a personal pet peeve).

I have also been experimenting with Kiro IDE. As an AI-powered fork of VS Code, it has left a very positive impression and is currently my go-to when I want to leverage LLMs directly in the workflow.

### Infrastructure & OS

- **Docker:** I containerize everything where it makes sense. I prefer clean, reproducible environments over "it works on my machine."
- **Operating Systems:** I am most at home on Linux or Windows 10. I am still finding it very difficult to reconcile with the UX changes in Windows 11, so I stick to stable, battle-tested environments whenever possible.
